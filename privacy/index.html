Pars Color Lab · Privacy

Your colors. Your device.

Privacy Policy for Pars Color Lab, developed by Amir Pakshir. Effective November 3, 2026. This policy describes version 1.0 (build 11) and will be updated if data practices change.

At a glance

Pars Color Lab does not collect personal data from the app for the developer. There are no advertising, tracking or third-party analytics SDKs, no account system and no app-managed cloud synchronization. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may provide the developer with anonymized crash reports and diagnostics; you can turn this off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

What stays on your device

Saved colors, formulas, estimates, lesson progress and preferences are stored locally on your device. Studio data — personal paints, Lab and spectral measurements and mix-correction records — is kept in a separate local file with its own backup in Settings. Images you choose or capture are processed locally for color sampling and photo analysis; the app does not store the photo itself, only the colors, palettes or recipes you save. The developer does not receive your photos or saved work.

Camera and photo access

Camera access is requested only when you choose to take a photo. The system photo picker lets you choose the image to use. These features are optional. You can manage camera access in the Settings app.

Sharing and backups

Files leave the app only when you choose a sharing or export action, such as a PDF report, CSV file, PNG study image or JSON backup, sent through Files, AirDrop, Mail or saved to your photo library. CSV and JSON files you import from Files are read on the device and are not uploaded. The recipient or service you select handles that copy under its own privacy practices. Device backups may be handled by Apple or your chosen backup service; the app does not operate its own cloud backup.

Subscriptions

Pars Color Lab Pro is an optional auto-renewable subscription sold by Apple ($1.99 per month or $19.99 per year in the US, with a one-week free trial for new subscribers). Apple processes the payment; the developer does not receive your payment details or Apple Account information. The app checks your subscription status on the device with Apple StoreKit. Manage or cancel the subscription in your Apple Account settings. Terms of Use: https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/

Support messages

If you email support, your email address, message and any attachments are received by the developer and the email provider. They are used to respond to and manage your request, not for advertising. Send only information necessary for the issue. You may request deletion of support correspondence, subject to any necessary record-keeping.

Your controls

You can delete saved items and revoke camera access. Deleting the app removes local app data; offloading does not. Exported files and device backups remain under your control and must be deleted separately. The developer cannot retrieve or restore local work that you have not shared.

External sources and this website

Lesson reference links open third-party websites with their own policies. This support website has no advertising or analytics scripts. Its hosting provider may process technical request information, such as IP address and browser details, to deliver and secure the pages. Your app data is not sent to this website.

Children and changes

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. These pages will display updated practices and an effective date when the policy changes.
